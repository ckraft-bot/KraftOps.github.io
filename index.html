Home

Despite being highly capable, competent, and brilliant, autistic individuals face an unemployment rate of ~80%. Every individual on the autism spectrum deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential and to experience meaning, dignity, and autonomy in their lives. Gaining employment is a critical step toward financial independence and greater personal freedom. If you are looking to secure employment, I am available to assist you with resume review, mock interviews, LinkedIn profile setup/optimization, and networking strategies.
